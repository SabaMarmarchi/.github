Saba Marmarchinia
Ph.D. Candidate
Department of Civil, Structural and Environmental Engineering
University at Buffalo

Saba earned her bachelor’s and master’s degrees from the University of Tehran. 
Her research focuses on probabilistic fault displacement hazard analysis and the statistical characterization of surface fault rupture. 
Her work aims to develop next-generation methodologies for estimating the hazard associated with principal and distributed fault ruptures and their implications for the seismic design of critical infrastructure. 
Her future research will focus on developing a framework for the combined assessment of ground-motion and fault-displacement hazards.
